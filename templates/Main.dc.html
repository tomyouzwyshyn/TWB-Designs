Mobile closed-chamber destruction of small arms ammunition.
Company

TWB Designs Inc. builds trailer-deployed closed-combustion systems that destroy small arms ammunition up to .50 calibre inside the perimeter of the organisation that owns it. In continuous service since 2001, co-developed with the Royal Canadian Mounted Police. Incorporated in Ontario; operating from Hamilton, Canada.

Core competencies
On-site destruction of expired, seized, surrendered and surplus small arms ammunition
Materials recovery — lead, antimony, brass and copper graded at the point of destruction
Chain-of-custody protocol and certificate of destruction
Operator training, qualification and sustainment
Equipment supply, field service and spares
Differentiators
Zero custody transfers. Material never leaves the owner's controlled area.
Same-day deployment. Towed on a Class V hitch; operational the day it arrives.
Operated by the customer. Two trained agency personnel; no contractor on site.
Not open burning. Closed, air-controlled chamber with emissions treatment (40 CFR 260.10).
