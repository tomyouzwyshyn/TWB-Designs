Stockpiles are being rebuilt faster than the capacity to retire them
Unlike a rifle or a hull, ammunition must be destroyed to be retired. Three demand pools are converging on a destruction base that was sized for peacetime.
$2.88T
World military expenditure 2025 — all-time record, 11th consecutive rise (SIPRI)⁹⁶

296,534 t
US munitions awaiting demilitarization, 4 Dec 2025 — >7,000 DODICs; Army forecasts generations rising FY27–31⁹⁸
Allied stockpile turnover
Europe doubled military spending 2016–25⁹⁶; NATO identifies $145bn of munitions requirements and Allied production is up ~sixfold⁹⁽. Every new round is a future destruction obligation; expiring and orphaned calibres accumulate as rearmament turns stockpiles over.
Post-conflict recovery
Ukraine’s explosive-hazard management need is put at US$27.6bn for 2026–2035 across ~133,000 km² (RDNA5)²⁰ — the first invoice of this war’s end-of-life, not the last. Clearance produces ordnance that still has to be destroyed.
Every agency, every year
Law-enforcement seizures, take-back events and expired duty ammunition accumulate in evidence rooms with no compliant disposal path — a persistent backlog in every jurisdiction served. The UN ran two procurements for mobile ammunition destruction in 2025 alone²¹.
The structural gap: the world’s demil capacity is fixed-plant, centralised and permitted over years. Ordnance is distributed, hazardous to move and accumulating now.

The opportunity · The gap
12 / 25
Destruction never industrialised — and the incumbent method is being regulated out
The status quo is a pit, a truck, or a pair of hands. Fixed plants cannot reach where the ordnance is.
Burn it in a pit
Open burning / open detonation — combustion with no containment and no emissions control. Roughly $750/ton²², and the EPA’s proposed rule (89 FR 19952) targets the 67 US facilities still doing it; a final rule is projected for late 2026²³.
Truck it away
Live ammunition loaded onto highways to reach a permitted depot. Every mile a diversion window; every transfer a custody break; every escort a cost the owner carries.
Do it by hand
Los Angeles County, 18 July 2025: three sheriff’s detectives killed processing seized ordnance²⁴. The default when nothing else is on site.
