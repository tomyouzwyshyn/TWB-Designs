# US conventional ammunition demilitarization backlog — extracted series

Source: US Army, *Procurement of Ammunition, Army* — President's Budget justification
books, FY2018 through FY2027. Budget line item **4099EP1700** ("EP1700" in the FY2018
book), *Conventional Munitions Demilitarization*. Account: **SMCA Resource, Recovery and
Disposition Account (B5A)**.

Every figure below is a direct text extraction from the primary PDF. None is interpolated.

## Caveats to state if challenged
- Snapshot dates are irregular (month-only before FY2024) and are inventory snapshots, not
  fiscal-year endpoints. Plotted at their actual dates; do not read as an annual rate.
- No snapshot exists between Nov 2019 and Mar 2021.
- The FY2018 book labels the line item EP1700, not 4099EP1700. Same line item.
- FY2019 and FY2020 books state no budget-year tonnage, only prior-year actual and
  current-year plan — those two years are omitted from the funded-plan table above.
- Years-to-clear (9.4) is ours: 296,534 ÷ 31,475. It assumes no new arrivals.
- Earlier context, different source: GAO-15-538 put the account at 529,373 short tons in
  February 2015.
