Overview

Why it exists

Some ground should not be walked and some water should not be dived until it is known to be clear. PICKET runs the unmanned platforms that go first: ground, surface, and underwater, searching, marking, and staging so the recovery crew never has to be the one that finds out the hard way.

0.1 / 0.3

Clears ground crews do not have to walk

Ground, surface, and underwater platforms search a site ahead of any crew, so exposure to unknown ordnance and contamination happens to a machine first.
