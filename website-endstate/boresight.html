Overview

Why it exists

A manifest tells you what is supposed to be there. BORESIGHT tells you what actually is. It reads every item on the line in real time, confirms it against expectation, and catches the exception before it reaches the chamber.

0.1 / 0.3

Confirms what is really on the line

Every item is read, classified, and counted as it moves, checked against the manifest in real time rather than reconciled afterward.
