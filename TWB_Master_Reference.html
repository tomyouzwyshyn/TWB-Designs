01 · The business

One machine, one wedge, two stories

TWB Designs Inc. (Hamilton, Ontario) builds Vault — a trailer-deployed, closed-chamber system that destroys small arms ammunition to .50 calibre, plus seized narcotics and other seized property, inside the owner's perimeter, same day, operated by the owner's own people. Held and operated under CLN (kept quiet in market — footer and diligence only) until it reaches scale to sell. The category is small arms ammunition destruction, deliberately not "demilitarization": demil is scored on tons/year, where TWB loses by 3× to 200×. The wedge is custody — every competitor moves the material. Hero line: "The property never leaves your custody."

Track A · Operating brand

Vault — the custody doctrine

  • Machine named Vault; eMACS/MACS retired in all market-facing material.
  • Buyers: law enforcement, defence, allied/NATO-forward. Narcotics appears as capability in LE copy only — never as a referenced past-performance claim.
  • Pitch net cost of destruction (price minus verified recovered value), never gross "cheaper" — OB/OD at ~$750/ton wins any raw per-ton fight.
  • Carried by: website, capability brief, templates, design system.

02 · The four claims

Always in this order

  1. Same day. On site. Your own people.Uncontested — the nearest comparable closed chamber states a three-week deployment on its own datasheet (Dynasafe SDC 1000, now citable).
  2. Zero transfers. Zero diversion windows.The custody wedge itself. Every handoff is a manifest, a truck, and an interval where the count can change.
  3. Recovered, not released.Domestic metal supply, not green virtue. Carries the cost-recovery component: Pb–Sb, brass, copper sold into existing secondary markets, netted against the destruction fee.
  4. Nobody stands over it.LASD, 18 July 2025 — three detectives killed processing seized ordnance by hand.

Register: pragmatic, protective, threat-framed. Hard-edged, never martial. Footage rule: machinery, material and procedure — never combat.

06 · Design system

The values every asset uses

Sage is the only hue — #66705F text-grade for all small type, #788274 (the client sample) for marks, borders, fills and large type, #F1F3F0 wash for proof surfaces. It must own whole surfaces, not just labels. The negative register (conventional disposal, superseded claims) is dark neutral #3D3D38 with dashed strokes — weight and form, never a traffic light. Single face: Helvetica Neue, weight climbing as size falls (400 display / 500 section / 600 sub-head); UI layer is IBM Plex Mono, uppercase, +.09em. Zero border radius; 6×6px corner brackets offset −3px are the motif; 120ms transitions; all motion inside prefers-reduced-motion. Light ground only — the system inverts on painted steel, never on screen. Statutory hazard marking is regulatory red/yellow and is never restyled. The React package mirrors all of this for Claude Design; tokens.css is imported, never re-typed.

07 · Risks & exposure

What could break this

  • Regulatory classification. "Two-stage combustion" wording may class Vault as an incinerator under 40 CFR 260.10 rather than a Subpart X unit — materially heavier permitting, decided partly by our own copy. Environmental counsel before any copy locks. The offsetting gift: the same section defines open burning as combustion without air control, containment or emissions control — a closed chamber is categorically not open burning, and that is citable.
  • The dioxin/recovery conflict (teardown's sharpest finding). The 850°F primary chamber sits at the top of the PCDD/PCDF de-novo synthesis window with copper — the most effective dioxin catalyst known — present in bulk by design. Running hotter destroys the brass recovery that funds claim 3. Emissions risk and the revenue model are in physical conflict until EPA Method 23 testing says otherwise. No further thermal claims before it.
  • US standing. The Canadian entity cannot hold SBA small-business status, an ATF FEL, or DDTC registration — claiming any is False Claims Act exposure. The CAGE sentence in CLAUDE.md is contested: DLA covers Canadian entities (NCAGE) and a Canadian firm can register in SAM and prime US federal work — the real barriers are set-asides and facility clearance. Fix the sentence. Canada IS a DFARS 225.872 qualifying country — checkable and underused. ITAR is better than assumed (s126.5 Canadian exemption applies to Cat V); SBIR/STTR is closed and must never be listed as a route.
  • CADS contradictions. The sister company's site sells "at your site or at ours" with per-km travel pricing — the opposite of the custody wedge — and states 100 lb/hr at 482/982°C where the deck states 60 lb/hr at 850/1750°F. Two brands, two spec sheets, one machine: still the single biggest brand liability.
  • The disclosure gap. The retired investor deck implied $235.9M of value over a real combined baseline of $862,856 CAD revenue / ~$235K EBITDA that only the memo disclosed. The rebuilt Track-B materials must never depend on a companion document to be accurate — and the $700K LOI acquisition basis (signed back, per D1) is the strongest fact in the package and should lead.
  • The wedge/roadmap conflict. Adding an energetics front end destroys the custody wedge (FDHS precedent: 10-day setup, 15 crew per shift, multi-container). Any roadmap step gets tested against claim 1 before it gets a slide.

  • Removed from the set: Chemring, Ordnance Holdings, PIKA, Tradebe, Veolia (Perma-Fix stays as a valuation comparable only). Dynasafe BACTEC is no longer Dynasafe — it is Igne, under Solix.
09 · Acquisition strategy

Tom's filter, and the shortlist it produced

Buy less direct competition, more artillery and recovery: large-calibre and artillery demil, metals recovery and reclamation, plant and equipment engineering, secure destruction for military and conflict customers. North America and Europe. Ranked: 1 · JAKUSZ (Poland — containerised disposal factories to 203mm+, succession signal) · 2 · Josef Meissner (Cologne — waterjet washout, TNT/HMX/RDX recovery, carve-out) · 3 · Alford Technologies / El Dorado Engineering (cleanest cap table / transportable furnace + RCRA permitting practice) · 4 · Interco Trading (the only firm publicly buying demilled ordnance; 10,000+ t non-ferrous/month) · 5 · Lacero Solutions / Gradient Technology (best Canadian fit, 45 min from Hamilton / the real Year-1 counterparty — its core patent expired May 2023, no licence needed). Pricing honesty: real deals cleared 1.5×–3.1× revenue and 9×–15× EBITDA; for sub-$20M founder businesses the honest frame is 4×–7× EBITDA. Three of the four recovered metals — copper, lead, antimony — are on the Final 2025 US Critical Minerals List; copper gilding metal has no published scrap index and is real, unpriced margin if segregated.
