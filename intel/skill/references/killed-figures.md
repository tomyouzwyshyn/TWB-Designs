# Killed figures — never publish these

Each entry is a figure that circulates widely and fails on contact with a primary source.
This list is cumulative. Add to it whenever verification kills something.

## Conflict figures

| Killed figure | Problem | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| Sudan "at least 59,000 killed" | Extrapolation from a Khartoum-state mortality study, not a body count | Cite IPC food-security figures and IOM displacement; say no verified national death toll exists |
| Gaza cumulative death toll since Oct 2023 | No permitted source carries a dated August 2026 snapshot; Wikipedia's "80,692+" has no retrievable as-of date | Use OCHA's since-ceasefire figures (1,259 killed / 4,148 injured, 14 Aug 2026) and attribute to Gaza MoH via OCHA |
| "4,300 killed in Lebanon since 2 March 2026" | Single-sourced to Lebanese authorities via one outlet | Attribute explicitly or omit; OHCHR Lebanon updates are the primary route |
| "1 million displaced in Lebanon" | Wikipedia aggregation to sources not opened | Omit until a UNHCR figure is obtained |
| Gaza reconstruction "$70bn" | Appears only in secondary reporting; no dated primary UN/World Bank RDNA document traced | Use UNDP's 61m tonnes of rubble and $110m immediate clearance requirement (20 Nov 2025) |
| Cobasna "Hiroshima-equivalent detonation" | Attributed to a 2015 Moldovan Academy of Sciences study that cannot be traced; physically implausible for 20,000 t of conventional ordnance | State the tonnage (up to 20,000 t) and that it is OSCE-unmonitored |
| "EU 155mm capacity reached 2 million rounds/year" | Not on the European Commission's own ASAP page; untraceable | Cite EDIP's €1.5bn programme and >€700m for counter-drone systems, missiles and ammunition (30 Mar 2026) |
| NATO ammunition production "up sixfold" | Political statement by the Secretary General with no published baseline | Cite the REPEAD figure: up to $145bn in shared munitions and air-defence requirements |
| PRIO "255,000 battle-related deaths in 2025" | Irreconcilable with UCDP; almost certainly a category artefact | UCDP's ~244,600 total organised-violence deaths, labelled as such |
| Any single DPRK→Russia shell figure | Estimates range 6–12 million, all from intelligence assessments, mutually inconsistent | Give the range and name the assessing service; never a single number |
| Mexico "homicides down 46%" / "cut by half" | Government SESNSP figure; methodology contested; INEGI cross-check unavailable | Attribute to the Mexican government explicitly, or omit |
| DRC "14,400 women raped" / "300,000 fled" at Uvira | Congolese government claims | Use the IPIS-reported mass grave finding (150+ bodies) |
| Burkinabè "400 militants killed" | Belligerent claim | Omit |
| Ukrainian/Russian personnel casualty claims | Belligerent claims from both sides | Use OHCHR civilian casualty data only |
| "242 Russian naval vessels destroyed" | Ukrainian claim relayed by ISW; implausible for major vessels | Omit entirely |
| Vectus Global "1,200+ killed by drone" in Haiti | Single non-wire source | Omit |
| Serbian ammunition to Ukraine "~€800m" | FT, June 2024, single source, two years stale | Cite the export halt (26 Jun 2025) instead |

## Corrections applied at verification (Edition 001, 25 Aug 2026)

*Note: these were applied in a second pass, after Edition 001 had already been issued and emailed.
The first issue went out without any verification pass having been run. See lessons.md.*

- Poland's Anti-Personnel Mine Ban Convention withdrawal took effect **20 February 2026**, not
  26 February. Estonia/Latvia/Lithuania: 27 Dec 2025. Finland: 10 Jan 2026. Current depositary
  tally: **162 states parties** — not the 164 that appears on unrevised factsheets.
- Türkiye's PKK reintegration law passed **10 August 2026** (468–88, six abstentions), not
  11 August. 11 August is the wire-report date.
- Iraq's "September 2026 militia weapons-surrender deadline" was **disavowed by the Iraqi
  government on 21–24 August 2026** — "September 30 is the date for the international coalition
  to depart, not the deadline to hand over weapons." No replacement date set. Do not publish the
  deadline.
- UNMAS oPt's $9.2m is an **additional** request to sustain the 2026 response, not the 2026
  requirement.
- EDIP's third funding category is **counter-drone systems**, not "bombs."
- The Gaza Board of Peace agreement provides for **storage rather than destruction**; it contains
  no explicit prohibition on destruction. Do not write "explicitly not destroyed."
- The 155mm production figures come from **Department of War** Inspector General report
  **DOWIG-2026-095** (the prefix is DOWIG, not DODIG), released 13 July 2026.
- Landmine Monitor's "civilians 90% of casualties" is qualified in the original as *where
  military or civilian status was known*. Unqualified, civilians are 86% of all 6,279.

## Methodology conflicts — never combine these series

- **Myanmar mine/ERW casualties.** UNICEF (own reporting network) and the Landmine Monitor
  (stricter verification) differ by roughly 30%. Use one, name it, never mix.
- **Ukraine contamination.** The Ukrainian government's ~139,000 km² is a *potentially hazardous
  area* survey figure. The Monitor's 105.88 km² is *confirmed* contamination. Different things.
- **NATO 2% GDP compliance.** SIPRI's methodology puts 22 of 29 European members at or above 2.0%
  in 2025; NATO's own report says all Allies met it. Both are correct as quoted. If they appear
  near each other, attribute each or the pair reads as a contradiction.

## Stale sources — date-stamp when using

- Small Arms Survey UEMS database: 674 incidents, 1979 – December 2024, last updated January 2025.
- US State Department conventional weapons destruction pages: FY2019–FY2020 vintage data.
- European Commission ASAP page: not updated past 2024.
- Landmine Monitor's most recent casualty and funding data is **2024 data**; the 2026 edition
  publishes in Nov/Dec. Anyone presenting "current" landmine figures is presenting 2024 numbers.

## Corrections from the second verification pass, 25 Aug 2026

- **SIPRI "+9.2% arms transfers" is a five-year comparison**, not an annual rise: the volume of
  transfers in 2021–25 was 9.2% higher than in 2016–20. Presenting it as a 2025 year-on-year
  increase is wrong. (SIPRI Fact Sheet, 9 Mar 2026.)
- **UCDP's 13 wars in 2025 equals the 2013 peak** — "no year since 1992 has had more." Not a
  clean "highest since 1992."
- **UCDP "second deadliest year since 1994"** could not be quoted verbatim from JPR 63(4). The
  244,600 figure is solid; the ranking is not. State the figure, not the rank.
- **NATO's $145bn** is an aggregated statement of allied *requirements* from the REPEAD process —
  not spending, budget or commitment — and covers deep precision strike and air surveillance as
  well as munitions and air defence.
- **EDIP has no single ammunition/counter-drone category.** €700m+ sits in production capacity
  support and €240m in joint procurement, under different rules.
- **Haiti's election is 13 December 2026** (runoff 21 Feb 2027), not January 2027.
- **The Kenscoff attack of 24 Aug 2026 killed at least 47** and abducted 50+, not 30.
- **Guatemala's denial of a joint-operations agreement followed** the mid-August Hegseth
  announcement; it did not precede it in May.
- **"Colombian combat operations rose from 447 to 563"** — untraceable. Removed.
- **Haiti "~500,000 illegal firearms in circulation"** — repeated by UNIDIR and InSight Crime but
  not traceable to any Haiti-specific survey. Do not use as a planning figure.
- **Colombia coca hectarage for 2024/2025** — no published UNODC survey exists. The last verified
  figure is 253,000 ha for 2023.
- **"Gangs control X% of Port-au-Prince"** — circulating figures run 70–90% from four UN-adjacent
  sources that disagree. Give the range, never a single percentage.
