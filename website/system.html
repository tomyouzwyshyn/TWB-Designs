The system

Vault

A closed, armoured, trailer-deployed destruction chamber for small arms ammunition up to .50 calibre.

Built to arrive behind a pickup truck.

Vault is a two-stage closed combustion system on a 26-foot trailer. It tows on a Class V hitch, runs on propane or diesel with an onboard generator, and needs a minimum standoff of 750 feet from occupied structures. It is designed around one constraint: that the organisation which owns the ammunition can destroy it without anyone else entering the process.

On throughput. Vault is rated at 60 lb/hr up to .50 calibre. We publish the hourly rate, not a daily figure — daily figures in this category assume shift lengths nobody runs. If your requirement is measured in tons per year, we are not the right supplier, and we will tell you so on the first call.

Recovery

Every round comes back as metal. None of it comes back as a part.

Combustion separates the round into its constituent metal streams, graded on site and ready for sale into existing secondary markets. Nothing recoverable leaves in a form that can be reassembled, resold as a component, or put back into circulation. This stream is the cost-recovery case: destruction priced net of returned metal, not gross.

Lead & antimony

Cores recover as a Pb–Sb alloy ingot — battery-grade feedstock sought by secondary lead smelters.

Cartridge brass

Among the highest-value non-ferrous scrap grades, recovered clean and separated.

Copper jackets

Separated automatically within the same cycle. No downstream sorting required.

Verification status. Historic operating data indicates recovery of approximately 97% of recyclable material per burn cycle. Independent verification in progress

Third-party measurement of both recovery yield and stack emissions has been commissioned and the reports will be published. Until they exist we present these as our own operating figures rather than verified performance — and we would encourage you to hold every supplier in this category to that standard.
