Operators

If your name is on the property record, this is built for you.

Four kinds of organisation buy this machine, for four reasons. The custody argument is the same in all four.

Law enforcement

Evidence custodians · armourers · range officers · EOD

Seized ammunition. Surrendered rounds from buy-backs and take-back days. Duty ammunition past service life. Range sweepings. Seized narcotics and other property ordered destroyed. None of it can be sold, none of it can be discarded, and every item is signed for by somebody.

Vault clears the locker inside your own compound, on a schedule you set, with no contractor entering the chain — and the recovered metal comes back against the cost of the programme, so the locker stops being a pure budget line. Usually procured on a blanket purchase order rather than a competition.

Commercial & industrial

Manufacturers · distributors · ranges · licensees

Off-specification production. Returned and expired stock. Discontinued lines. Ammunition that must be destroyed for legal or liability reasons and currently has to be shipped to a third party to do it.

Recovered metal offsets a meaningful share of disposal cost, and the material never leaves your facility or your insurance perimeter.

Defence — installation level

Range control · ammunition supply points · base EOD

The objection to closed disposal raised most often is that preprocessing and consolidation expose personnel to more handling risk, not less. That objection is correct — and it is an objection to fixed-site closed disposal.

A chamber that arrives at the point of storage removes the consolidation step entirely. The material is processed where it already sits, by people already cleared to handle it.

Allied & international

Partner-nation forces · stockpile reduction

Small arms ammunition is the stream alternative-technology programmes consistently score as out of scope. It is also among the most divertible material in any national stockpile, and the stream most often burned in the open for want of an alternative.

Deployed with allied and partner-nation forces across three continents. Nordic and Baltic distribution through Military Equipment Denmark A/S.

When we are the wrong answer.

Vault is a small arms system. If your requirement is artillery, bombs, rocket motors, bulk propellant, or destruction measured in tons per year, we are not competitive and we will say so on the first call rather than the third.

We would rather lose a bid we cannot serve than win one we cannot deliver. In this category a supplier who over-promises creates a safety problem, not just a commercial one.
