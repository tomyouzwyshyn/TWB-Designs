Contracting

What we hold, and what we don’t.

Everything on this page is verifiable in a government database. What is pending is listed as pending, because you can check.

TWB Designs Inc. is a Canadian company, incorporated in Ontario and operating from Hamilton. A US entity is in formation. Until that entity exists and is registered, TWB does not hold a US CAGE code, US small-business status, an ATF Federal Explosives Licence, or a DDTC registration — and does not claim them.

TWB is not eligible for US small-business set-asides. Under 13 CFR 121.105 a small business concern must have a place of business in the United States. We state this proactively so no contracting officer spends time on it.

Acquisition routes

Qualifying country status

Canada is a qualifying country under DFARS 225.872. Canadian end products are exempt from the Buy American statute and the Balance of Payments Program in DoD acquisitions, and are eligible under the Trade Agreements Act.

Canadian Commercial Corporation

Under DFARS 225.870 the Government of Canada guarantees CCC’s contractual commitments to the US Government. Engagement underway

Teaming & subcontract

Where prime award is unavailable to a foreign entity, TWB supplies equipment, training and sustainment under a US prime. We will say plainly which route fits your requirement.

Export control

TWB maintains an export compliance programme aligned to Canada’s Export and Import Permits Act and the Controlled Goods Program. DDTC registration under 22 CFR Part 122 applies to persons engaged in the business in the United States and will be held by the US entity upon formation.

We do not describe ourselves as “ITAR certified” — no such certification exists. Detailed technical data is released only under appropriate export authorisation and a signed NDA, which is why full engineering documentation is not published here.
